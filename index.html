About

Built by engineers. For real-world use.

CBINNOV INC Research & Development was founded in 2020 in Montréal, Québec, Canada, by a group of engineers who couldn't find the product they were expecting on the market. So they simply decided to design and build it.

Today, CBINNOV develops brands and delivers high-quality, efficient products — engineered to be accessible to everyone.

  • 01

    Engineering-first

    Every product starts on the bench — designed, measured, and validated before it ships.

  • 02

    Quality & efficiency

    We build for longevity and real-world performance, not spec sheets.

  • 03

    Made for everyone

    Professional-grade products, priced and designed for broad accessibility.
